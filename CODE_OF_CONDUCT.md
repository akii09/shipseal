# Code of Conduct

Shipseal is an open project. Everyone taking part in it, through issues, pull requests, or code
review, is expected to keep it that way.

## What we expect

Be respectful. Give feedback that targets the work, not the person. Assume good intent,
especially from first-time contributors. If you make a mistake, own it and move on.

Most of what arrives here is a bug report about a wrong number on a card or a render that does
not match someone's brand. Treat those as the useful signal they are, whoever sends them.

## What we don't accept

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostile behavior
- Discriminatory or demeaning language of any kind
- Unwanted sexual language, imagery, or attention
- Intimidation, threats, or deliberate disruption
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project exists: GitHub issues, pull requests, and any other official
channel. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Email <akashpise588@gmail.com>. Include enough context to review it fairly: links, screenshots,
and roughly when it happened. Reports are read privately.

A security vulnerability is a separate process. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove someone, depending on the
severity and the pattern of behavior. The goal is to keep the project safe and constructive,
rather than to punish.
